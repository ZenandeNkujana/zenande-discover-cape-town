Cape Town

The Mother City

A city of beauty, culture and diversity.

Landmarks

Table Mountain

Table Mountain is one of Cape Town's most recognisable landmarks. Its unique flat top rises above the city and offers beautiful views of Cape Town, the ocean and the surrounding mountains. Visitors can reach the top by hiking or taking the cable car.

V&A Waterfront

The V&A Waterfront is a lively destination built around Cape Town's harbour. It brings together restaurants, shops, markets, museums, and attractions such as the Two Oceans Aquarium. It is a wonderful place for visitors and locals to enjoy the city's atmosphere.

Cape Point

Cape Point is known for its dramatic cliffs, beautiful ocean views and historic lighthouse. It forms part of Table Mountain National Park and offers walking trails and spectacular coastal scenery. It is a wonderful place to appreciate the natural beauty of the Cape Peninsula.

Food Culture

Cape Town has a diverse food culture that brings together different flavours, traditions and tastes. From the famous Cape Town Gatsby and traditional Cape Malay dishes to sushi and Nigerian food such as egusi, there is a wide variety of food to explore. The city's multicultural communities make its food scene unique, offering something for people with different tastes and preferences.

Activities To Do In Cape Town

  • Visit the beautiful beaches, including Camps Bay, Clifton and Muizenberg.
  • Hike Table Mountain, Lion's Head or Signal Hill.
  • Visit the Two Oceans Aquarium and discover marine life.
  • Visit Robben Island and learn about South Africa's history.
  • Explore Kirstenbosch National Botanical Garden and its beautiful gardens.

These are just a few of the many wonderful experiences Cape Town has to offer!
